# Observatory Product Charter v1

Status: **Frozen architecture baseline**. Changes require an explicit research
decision and must preserve reproducibility and exploratory boundaries.

## Purpose

Observatory maximizes observability, not conclusions. It organizes market and
company state, pair relationships, timing structure, industry/market context,
dated events, visual evidence, deterministic descriptive measurements, and
human notes so a researcher can observe, hypothesize, challenge, identify
missing evidence, and optionally export a question to a separate formal
research pipeline.

It is a discovery environment, not a strategy-validation environment.

## Permitted capabilities

- Fetch/cache sourced data and preserve provenance.
- Compute deterministic descriptive statistics and fixed visual comparisons.
- Display point-in-time company, industry, market, and event context.
- Store separate observations, hypotheses, counter-hypotheses, and evidence
  questions.
- Export a candidate hypothesis package without testing it.

## Prohibited capabilities

No backtesting, threshold/parameter optimization, pair-profitability ranking,
trading signals, alpha validation, causal inference, predictability claims,
good/bad pair classification, strategy selection, performance grid search, or
automatic conversion of exploratory material into labels, prediction targets,
confirmatory evidence, or production logic.

## Product equations

Machine Measurement != Human Observation. Human Observation != Hypothesis.
Hypothesis != Validated Relationship. Validated Relationship != Trading
Opportunity.
